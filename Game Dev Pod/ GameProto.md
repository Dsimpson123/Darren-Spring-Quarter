Medium: We made a real-life game where people move around and play outside.

Format: It is a competitive game where players get eliminated until one winner is left.

Objective: The goal is to reach the finish line without getting caught moving on “red light.”

Mechanics: Players move on “green light” and must freeze on “red light,” with extra rules added.

Turn Order: One person is the caller, and everyone else follows their commands until someone wins.

Goals: The goal is to react fast, stay focused, and try to win the game.

Challenge: A challenge was making new rules without making the game too confusing.

Most Fun Aspect: The most fun part was coming up with new ideas and testing the game.
