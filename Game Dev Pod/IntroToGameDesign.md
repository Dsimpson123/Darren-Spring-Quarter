In your own words, define what a game is. 
A game is an activity where players follow rules, complete challenges, and work toward a goal while having fun.

What is Game Design? 
Game design is the process of creating and planning how a game works.

What is the MDA framework? What are its parts? Define each.
The MDA Framework is a way to analyze and understand games. MDA stands for: Mechanics

-Mechanics are the rules and systems of the game. These include things like scoring, movement, abilities, timers, health systems, or any rule players must follow.
Dynamics:
-Dynamics are how the game behaves when players interact with the mechanics. This includes the strategies, teamwork, competition, or challenges that happen during gameplay.
-Aesthetics are the emotions and experiences players feel while playing the game. Examples include excitement, tension, fun, fear, achievement, or curiosity.
